Capacity Banner — Design Approaches

A persistent banner showing your weekly occupation based on assigned ticket estimates

A

Compact Progress Bar

A single-line banner below the nav tabs. Shows a progress bar with "Estimated: 32h / 40h (80%)" plus color coding: green ≤80%, orange 80-100%, red >100%.

Preview
Capacidad Semanal
80%
32h / 40h ● OK

Pros

  • Minimal space — always visible
  • Instant read on load status

Cons

  • No per-ticket breakdown visible
B

Expandable Banner

Same compact bar as A, but clicking it expands to show a breakdown: each ticket with its estimate, status, and contribution to the total. Collapsible to stay out of the way.

Pros

  • Compact by default, detailed on demand
  • See which tickets are eating capacity

Cons

  • Slightly more complex to build
C

Dual Bar (Estimated vs Logged)

Two stacked progress bars: one for estimated hours (from ticket estimates), one for logged hours (from worklogs). Shows both planned load and actual progress side by side.

Pros

  • See planned vs actual at a glance
  • Spot if you're behind or ahead

Cons

  • Takes more vertical space
  • Two bars may feel busy for a banner
